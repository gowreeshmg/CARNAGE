THE TECHNOLOGY

Algorithms Behind
The Wheel

Deep Neural Networks

Multi-layer convolutional networks process 60 frames per second, identifying objects with superhuman accuracy using ResNet-50 and YOLO v8 architectures.

92% Detection Accuracy

SLAM Navigation

Simultaneous Localization and Mapping builds centimeter-accurate 3D maps in real time. Extended Kalman Filter maintains position even in GPS-denied tunnels and garages.

±2cm Positioning Accuracy

Reinforcement Learning

Vehicles train across billions of simulated miles using reward-based RL. Tesla FSD shadow mode continuously learns from 5 million real-world fleet vehicles.

78% Edge Case Handling

Sensor Fusion Engine

Kalman filters and Bayesian probabilistic models merge LiDAR, RADAR, ultrasonic and camera streams into one unified world model updated at 100Hz.

96% Fusion Reliability

V2X Communication

Vehicle-to-Everything protocols enable sub-10ms communication with traffic infrastructure, other vehicles, and cloud AI systems via 5G-V2X and DSRC.

< 10ms Latency

Safety Arbitration

Multi-layer architectures with triple-redundant decision trees trigger AI overrides in microseconds. ISO 26262 ASIL-D certified with formal mathematical safety proofs.
